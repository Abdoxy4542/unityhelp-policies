UnityHelp Mobile - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 11, 2025
Effective Date: December 11, 2025

1. Introduction

UnityHelp Mobile ("we," "our," or "the App") is a humanitarian data collection application designed to support aid workers, NGOs, and UN agencies in collecting, managing, and analyzing field assessment data in humanitarian crisis situations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the UnityHelp Mobile application.

Contact Information:
Organization: UnityHelp Platform
Email: privacy@unityhelp.org
Website: https://unityhelp.org
Support: support@unityhelp.org

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you register for UnityHelp Mobile, we collect:

  • Name (first and last name)
  • Email address
  • Organization affiliation
  • Role (GSO, NGO User, UN User, Cluster Lead, Admin)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Preferred language (English or Arabic)

2.2 Location Data

For field assessment purposes, we collect:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) when you create reports or assessments
  • Location names (site names, locality, state information)
  • Location access is required for the app to function properly in the field
Why we collect location data:
  • To accurately tag assessment sites for coordination purposes
  • To map humanitarian needs geographically
  • To ensure data integrity and prevent duplicate entries
  • To comply with humanitarian reporting standards (SPHERE, Cluster guidelines)

2.3 Media Content

We collect media files you create or attach:

  • Photos taken during field assessments
  • Voice recordings for verbal reports and assessments
  • Images uploaded from your device gallery

Purpose: Document field conditions, evidence for humanitarian response, and faster data collection.

2.4 Assessment and Report Data

We collect information you submit through the app:

  • Field reports (text descriptions, site conditions, observations)
  • Assessment responses (survey answers, sectoral data)
  • Timestamps (when reports/assessments were created)
  • Offline data (stored locally until internet connection is restored)

2.5 Technical Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system version, device ID)
  • App version and build number
  • Network status (online/offline mode)
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • App usage statistics (feature usage, session duration)

Purpose: To improve app performance, fix bugs, and optimize the user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

We use your information to:

  1. Provide humanitarian services: Enable field data collection, coordinate response efforts, generate reports, and share critical information with authorized organizations.
  2. Authenticate and authorize: Verify your identity, provide access to assigned sites, and ensure data security.
  3. Improve the app: Fix bugs, develop new features, optimize performance, and enhance user experience.
  4. Communicate with you: Send updates, notify about maintenance, respond to support requests, and share resources.

3.2 Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Authorized humanitarian organizations: NGOs, UN agencies, and cluster leads for coordination
  • Government authorities: When required by law or for humanitarian operations
  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, database services, analytics platforms (all bound by confidentiality)
We will NEVER:
  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with commercial advertisers
  • Use your data for purposes unrelated to humanitarian operations

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where We Store Your Data

  • Primary servers: Secure cloud infrastructure (encrypted storage)
  • Backup locations: Redundant encrypted backups in multiple regions
  • Local device storage: Offline data encrypted on your device until synced

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3)
  • Encryption at rest: Database encryption using AES-256
  • Access controls: Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication
  • Regular security audits: Quarterly penetration testing
  • Secure authentication: Token-based with automatic expiration

4.3 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained as long as account is active
  • Inactive accounts: Data retained for 2 years after last login
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Legal requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access Your Data

You have the right to view all data, request a copy in machine-readable format, and review your submissions.

How to access: Profile → Settings → Download My Data

5.2 Correct Your Data

You can update your profile, edit reports within 24 hours, and request corrections.

How to correct: Edit in the app or contact support@unityhelp.org

5.3 Delete Your Data

You can delete individual reports, request account deletion, or remove yourself from operations.

Important: Deleting your account permanently removes all data. This cannot be undone.

How to delete: Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or email privacy@unityhelp.org

5.4 Restrict Processing

Request to stop using data for certain purposes, limit access, or opt out of communications.

How to restrict: Contact privacy@unityhelp.org

5.5 Data Portability

Request a copy of your data in portable format or transfer to another service.

How to export: Profile → Settings → Export Data

5.6 Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent by disabling permissions or uninstalling the app.

Note: Some features require certain permissions. Withdrawing may limit functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

UnityHelp Mobile is not intended for individuals under 18 years. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

If we discover inadvertent collection, we will delete that information immediately. Contact privacy@unityhelp.org if you believe a child has provided information.

7. International Data Transfers

UnityHelp operates globally and may transfer data to countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate protection through standard contractual clauses and compliance frameworks.

Processing countries: European Union (primary), United States (backup), Regional servers in operation countries

8. Third-Party Services

UnityHelp Mobile integrates with:

  • Google Maps / Mapbox: Map display and location services
  • OpenAI: AI-powered assessment assistance (anonymized data)
  • AWS/Google Cloud: Secure storage and hosting (encrypted)
  • Sentry: Crash reporting and error monitoring (anonymized)

These services are bound by their own privacy policies.

9. Cookies and Tracking

UnityHelp Mobile does not use cookies or tracking for advertising.

We use: Authentication tokens, anonymized app analytics, and error tracking.

Disable analytics: Profile → Settings → Privacy → App Analytics

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. You'll be notified via in-app notification, email, and the updated date at the top.

Continued use after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
